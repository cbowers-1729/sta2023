Draws both groups as cumulative curves on one panel. Unequal sample sizes and bin width are not a factor, so it is often the fairer comparison when one group is much smaller.
Unchecked (default): Holm-adjusted p-values with unadjusted per-comparison intervals. Checked: a single-step Bonferroni adjustment applied to both, so all intervals hold jointly at the confidence level above.
The direction refers to the first-listed group or the test value (see the R console for R's exact statement). One-sided choices change the test's p-value and its own interval; effect-size intervals remain two-sided.
